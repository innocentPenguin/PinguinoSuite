# Helper public summaries

Safe to share with any viewer. Each `## Title` section becomes one entry in the PUBLIC knowledge pack (Tools\build-helper-pack.py). No internals, no names, no secrets, no links except public ones. Keep each section short.

## How to join the viewer games
Register once with !register YourEpicName, then say "add me to the queue" or use !join when the queue is open. When you are picked, search the private match name in Rocket League. !queue shows the line, !pos shows your spot.

## Where to find every command
The full list of chat commands, with search, is at https://innocentpenguin.com/commands (also https://innocentpenguin.com/help).

## Leaderboards and profiles
Top gifters, bitters, yappers and watch streaks, all time or by month: https://innocentpenguin.com/leaderboards. Your own profile and stats: https://innocentpenguin.com/members.

## Emotes
The channel's 7TV emotes are listed and searchable at https://innocentpenguin.com/emotes. !emote shows how often an emote is used.

## Strikes and chat rules
Be kind, no spam, no hate. Mods give strikes for rule breaking and strikes fade by one per day. The rules and common questions are at https://innocentpenguin.com/faq.

## Opting out of the bots
Say !unping to stop the bots pinging you, and !forgetme to have the chat bot forget what it learned about you.

## Reminders and fun commands
!remindme 30m take a break sets a reminder in chat, !reminders lists them, !cancelreminder removes one. !8ball asks the penguin a yes or no question.

## Getting help from a person
For anything the helper cannot answer, ask in chat and a mod or Ivan will see it. The helper cannot ban, time out or change settings.

## Stream schedule
Ivan streams every day at 5:00 PM Central. Rocket League with viewers comes first for a couple of hours, then other games (Minecraft Monday, Fortnite Friday, Scary Saturday). The full schedule is on the Twitch channel's Schedule tab: https://twitch.tv/innocentpenguin_/schedule

## Joining the Minecraft server from a console
Consoles join through Bedrock Connect. The steps are on its official page: https://github.com/Pugmatt/BedrockConnect then add the server address and Bedrock port from !ip.

## Asking the helper to do an action
The helper only answers questions. It cannot change the title, ban, mod or unmod anyone, release held messages, or change settings. For those, ask a mod in chat.

## Leaving the queue
Type !leave to take yourself out of the queue, and !pos to see your spot.

## Your points
Type !points to see your points. The leaderboards are at https://innocentpenguin.com/leaderboards.

## Joined players who played a game (the recycler)
Players without a strike can stay in the queue and are put back in line after they play. !queue shows the line.

## Customs time out
A custom game times out after 15 minutes of inactivity.

## Leaving the tournament wheel
Type !notme to take yourself off the wheel. !leave is only for the custom queue and does not touch the wheel.

## Languages
The helper answers in English and in Mexican Spanish. Ask it in either language and it answers in kind, for example "¿Cómo me uno a la cola?" or !helper ¿a qué hora transmite Ivan?

## Mistyped commands
If you mistype a command by one letter, the helper figures out what you meant. Info commands like !pos or !queue just run. For !join and !leave it asks "Did you mean !join?" and waits for your yes (sí in Spanish).
